Welcome. This project introduces major schools of communication theory in plain language and applies them to communication challenges that people face today, especially in AI-shaped contexts.

<div class="callout" role="note" aria-label="Project goal">
The goal is to make the commonplace visible by giving non-specialists practical theory tools for diagnosing communication problems.
</div>

## What you will find here

- Short, accessible pages focused on one analytical lens at a time.
- Starter explanations you can expand with your own examples and arguments.
- A practical page translating concepts into daily and professional use.
- A transparent page documenting the website build process.

## How to use this site

Start with the **Transmission View** page and move through each lens in sequence, or jump directly to the page that matches your communication problem.

This boilerplate is intentionally lightweight so you can replace, expand, or remove any section as you develop your own content.

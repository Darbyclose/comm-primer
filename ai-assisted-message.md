  <div class="kicker">THE EVERYDAY PROBLEM / 01</div>

  <h2>When does helpful become too helpful?</h2>

  <p class="lead">AI makes it easier than ever to find the right words. But what happens when we stop coming up with those words ourselves?</p>

  <p>Think about how often we use AI without really thinking about it. We ask ChatGPT to write emails, come up with Instagram captions, brainstorm ideas, and even help us figure out what to say to people we care about. And honestly, I get it. Sometimes you know exactly what you mean but have no idea how to put it into words.</p>

  <p>But I think there's a difference between using AI to help express something and letting it do all the expressing for us.</p>

  <h2>What are we actually giving up?</h2>

  <p>Imagine it's your best friend's birthday. You ask ChatGPT to write a thoughtful message, copy it, and send it. The message sounds great. It's sweet, well-written, and says all the right things.</p>

  <p>But how much of that message actually came from you?</p>

  <p>That's what interests me about AI and communication. Sometimes the process of figuring out what to say is part of what makes something meaningful. The awkward wording, the inside jokes, and even the mistakes can make a message feel personal.</p>

  <p>And this isn't just about texting. Creativity is part of everyday communication. We use it when we tell stories, make jokes, comfort someone, or figure out how to handle a difficult conversation.</p>

  <h2>AI isn't just another keyboard.</h2>

  <p>In <em>The Uninvited Author</em>, Carlos Evia and Sarah O'Keefe describe AI as an "uninvited algorithmic author" (9).</p>

  <p>I think that's a really interesting way to look at it. A keyboard lets me type what I want to say. AI can suggest what I should say, how I should say it, and sometimes even give me the idea in the first place.</p>

  <p>That means AI isn't always just helping us communicate. It can actually influence the message we're creating.</p>

  <div class="callout">
    <strong>The big question:</strong>
    Is AI helping us find our voices, or are we becoming too comfortable letting it speak for us?
  </div>

  <h2>It's not all bad.</h2>

  <p>I don't think using AI automatically makes something fake or uncreative. It can be genuinely helpful, especially when you're stuck or struggling to explain something.</p>

  <p>But I do think we should pay attention to how much we're relying on it. If we use AI for every idea, every message, and every difficult conversation, are we missing out on part of the creative process?</p>

  <p>That's what the rest of this primer explores. Communication isn't just about getting the words right. It's about the meaning, intention, and human connection behind them.</p>

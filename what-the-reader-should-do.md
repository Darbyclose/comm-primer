This page translates theory into action. Its purpose is to help readers apply communication theory tools in real personal and professional situations.

## Practical guidance framework

1. **Name the problem type**: delivery failure, meaning conflict, or power conflict.
2. **Choose the lens**: transmission, semiotic, encoding/decoding, or intention analysis.
3. **Test assumptions**: identify what each lens reveals and hides.
4. **Adjust practice**: revise message design, context framing, channel strategy, and feedback loops.

## Everyday use

Use this page for checklists, reflection prompts, and scenario-based guidance that readers can apply immediately in relationships, workplaces, and online platforms.

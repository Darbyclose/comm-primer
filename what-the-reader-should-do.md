This page translates theory into action. Use it whenever an AI tool drafts, summarizes, translates, or changes a message with real consequences.

## Practical guidance framework

1. **Name the human goal**: What should the other person understand, feel, or be able to do?
2. **Add the missing context**: What relationship, history, identity, or power difference will shape reception?
3. **Inspect the signs**: Which words sound like judgment, certainty, urgency, or authority?
4. **Imagine multiple readings**: What is a generous, negotiated, and resistant interpretation?
5. **Choose the channel**: Match the channel to the emotional and relational stakes.
6. **Invite a response**: A communication is not complete when the draft is generated; it needs a feedback path.

## Everyday use

### A 30-second send check

- Could the recipient tell what happened, rather than guess what I think of them?
- Have I separated observation from evaluation?
- Would I say this out loud in the same relationship?
- Have I left a real opening for correction or disagreement?
- If the AI draft disappeared, could I explain why every sentence belongs?

If the answer to the last question is no, the message is not finished.

This page develops interpretation through Umberto Eco's model of intention and extends it with **intentio algorithmi** to describe algorithmic influence.

## Eco's triad (starter framing)

- **Intentio auctoris**: what the author intended.
- **Intentio operis**: what the text structure supports.
- **Intentio lectoris**: what the reader brings to interpretation.

## Extending the framework

In AI environments, recommendation systems, ranking logic, and generation constraints shape what appears and how it is read. The proposed **intentio algorithmi** names this additional layer of non-human intentional force.

Use this page to test where classical intention frameworks still work and where algorithmic mediation demands conceptual expansion.

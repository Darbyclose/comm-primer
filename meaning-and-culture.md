This page focuses on semiotics and cultural meaning-making. Instead of asking only whether a message arrived, this lens asks how signs produce meaning for different audiences.

## What semiotics adds

- Meanings are socially produced, not fixed once and for all.
- The same image, phrase, or interface cue can mean different things in different contexts.
- Cultural codes shape interpretation before explicit argument even begins.

## Why this matters now

In AI-mediated communication, symbols are generated, remixed, and circulated quickly. Semiotics helps explain why misunderstanding can grow even when message delivery is technically successful.
